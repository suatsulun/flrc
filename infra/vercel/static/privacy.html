FL-ReportCard public demo

Privacy policy

Updated 21 September 2026. This notice covers the public demo at flrc.suatsulun.com, operated by Suat Sülün. It does not describe private school installations.

Google sign-in

We request Google OpenID Connect sign-in scopes (openid, email, and profile) to verify that you control a verified Google account. Google sends identity claims to the server during sign-in. Public visitor mode does not save your Google email address, name, picture, raw Google account identifier, or Google access and refresh tokens in the application database.

To recognize a returning visitor during their temporary session, we store a keyed, one-way hash of the Google account identifier, linked to a randomly named demo user and an expiry time. This is pseudonymous identity data, not anonymous data. Google sign-in is not used to access Gmail, Drive files, contacts, or calendars.

Sessions and demo activity

An essential HttpOnly cookie carries a signed session identifier; the session is held on the server in Redis. Visitor accounts last at most 24 hours. The application stores your changes to the fictional school, grade audit records, and export job metadata under your demo user. Generated workbook downloads expire after 24 hours. Directly generated PDF reports are returned to your browser; files you download remain on your device until you delete them.

The demo is shared: other visitors can inspect or change its school data. Do not enter real student or teacher information, personal signatures, confidential comments, or other sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

When the last session ends, or an expired account is encountered, the application removes its identity hash and deactivates the visitor record. The scheduled daily reset restores a synthetic baseline and removes visitor records, edits, and generated jobs from the active demo database. It also revokes sessions. The target reset time is midnight in Europe/Istanbul; free-hosting delays may cause a later catch-up reset. Infrastructure logs and provider recovery history may persist beyond the active database reset under each provider's retention practices.

Service providers and sharing

Google handles authentication. Vercel serves the website, Render runs the API and export worker, Neon stores application data, Upstash stores sessions and queued jobs, Cloudflare supplies domain DNS, and GitHub Actions schedules operations. Hosting providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses and timestamps to deliver and secure their services. Google identity data is used for authentication and temporary account continuity, not advertising, sale, or model training. Demo edits are visible to other visitors as described above.

Your choices and contact

You can explore the public information pages without signing in. Sign out to end a device's session and revoke the app's connection from your Google account settings. For private sign-in or deletion questions, contact the support email shown on the Google consent screen. The maintainer is Suat Sülün; general project questions can be raised in the public issue tracker. Never post account credentials or personal school data in an issue.
