FL-ReportCard

A complete fictional school, ready to explore.

Try the teacher and administrator tools for language assessment, school-year management, and printable report cards.

The demo contains 2023-2024, 2024-2025, and 2025-2026 as archived years, plus an editable 2026-2027 year. Explore classes and student histories, change assessment scores and comments, print English, German, and French reports, or export a year's workbook.

One shared practice school

All names, classes, grades, and comments in the baseline are fictional. Every visitor uses the same database and may see other visitors' edits. Use synthetic data only: do not enter real student information, private notes, signatures, or other personal data.

Google sign-in gives you a temporary administrator account for up to 24 hours. The demo is designed to restore its original dataset every day at midnight in Istanbul (Europe/Istanbul). Saved and unsaved changes are discarded, and visitors sign in again. A sleeping free service may take about a minute to wake up.

Your Google account

Google verifies your identity. The public visitor mode uses a temporary pseudonymous account and a keyed hash of your Google account identifier; it does not save your Google email, name, picture, or raw account identifier. Read the privacy policy before signing in.
